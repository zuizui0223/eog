## Current method-validation split

The active EOG claim is a conditional, set-valued biogeographic inference claim. Future benchmarks must distinguish two different questions:

1. **Primary identity-preserving inferential value** — can independent evidence discriminate a consequence preserved by exact world identity but erased by a predeclared compression of the same worlds?
2. **Secondary predictive added value** — does exact world identity improve held-out predictive loss over stated same-world and external comparators?

Do not use predictive superiority as the only validity test of a set-valued uncertainty representation. Conversely, do not call a structural distinction predictive superiority unless held-out prediction actually improves.

## World-universe benchmark rule

A benchmark world family must declare whether each dimension is:

- natural/process uncertainty; or
- analyst-choice uncertainty.

Quantile thresholds, product choices and preprocessing variants are analyst-choice sensitivity worlds unless externally calibrated as biological parameters. Do not turn them into dispersal or tolerance constants by interpretation alone.

Every robust/exclusion benchmark must state its finite coverage certificate and, when used ecologically, why the world universe is adequate for the intended claim.

## Validation-unit rule

The held-out unit must match the generalisation target. Species-island rows nested inside a small number of islands are not independent island replicates.

A high bootstrap replicate count does not increase the number of independent units. Small-cluster confirmatory intervals/tests require pre-outcome design-specific calibration; otherwise they are descriptive.

## Frozen empirical/reproduction material

Older A-Islands, Tanzania, Finland and other benchmark files may remain because frozen workflows/manuscripts/evidence ledgers depend on them. Their presence does not make them active architecture.

Do not physically delete older benchmark code solely because its narrative is obsolete; first verify that no frozen reproduction path or fingerprint depends on it.

## Cleanup rule

- Keep one active narrative in the canonical docs.
- Keep synthetic comparators small and claim-specific.
- Keep system-specific empirical code out of production APIs.
- Preserve adverse, null, blocked and indeterminate evidence.
- Do not repair a blocked frozen confirmation on the same opened dataset.
- Do not add a comparator merely because another adjacent method can be named.

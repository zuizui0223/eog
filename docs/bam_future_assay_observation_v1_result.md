### Interpretation

This does **not** mean the assay process became identified.

Instead, the two observed assay codes jointly form a signature such that no
target-discordant pair of joint ecological/observation hypotheses shares the same
signature.

Thus the relevant condition is weaker than observation-world identification:

> **the observation process may remain unresolved while the ecological target is
> robustly identified.**

This is a second target-specific identifiability phenomenon, now on the observation
side of the problem.

# Original EOG future-proof evidence leverage — v9 result

## Result

All seven preregistered hypotheses were supported.

The central result is:

> **The measurement that best reduces current world uncertainty is often not the
> measurement that best protects ecological conclusions against future world
> expansion.**

## Immediate versus future value

The B0 reference universe fixes the truth barrier level and q70 analyst rule, while it
can still contain multiple candidate sources.

Therefore:

- source measurement can contract the current B0 fiber;
- barrier measurement has zero current B0 contraction;
- analyst-rule validation has zero current B0 contraction.

Mean current contraction:

- source: **0.493**;
- barrier: **0**;
- analyst rule: **0**.

Yet mean gain in future world-growth certificate lifetime was:

- source: **+0.050**;
- barrier: **+0.117**;
- analyst rule: **+0.097**.

Thus evidence can have high **future-only** value even when it tells us nothing new
about the current B0 world set.

## Objective disagreement

The tie-broken action optimizing immediate B0 contraction differed from the action
optimizing mean future certificate lifetime in **178/272** eligible rows.

There were **205/272** rows where a zero-current-contraction barrier or analyst
diagnostic still produced positive future-proofing value.

And in **178 rows**, one of those zero-current-effect diagnostics was itself the
future-proof-optimal action.

## No universal measurement order

Future-proof-optimal actions:

- source: **94** rows;
- barrier: **91**;
- analyst rule: **87**.

So future-proof evidence design is not a fixed recipe such as “always identify the
source first.”

The optimal diagnostic depends on which future candidate worlds threaten the current
certificate set.

## Certificates can become immortal within the frozen growth pool

Across the panel, truth-consistent diagnostics converted **1,585** baseline
certificates from K>0 to K=0.

That means one diagnostic measurement could remove every threatening world in the
declared future-growth pool for those certificates.

No action ever reduced normalized certificate lifetime.

## Relation to the target-aware adaptive planner

The earlier target-aware planner showed that one should stop once the ecological target
is identified, even if multiple worlds or sources remain.

v9 adds a second criterion:

> **If the target is already identified, evidence may still be worth collecting when it
> protects that target against a declared class of future world expansion.**

So EOG evidence planning now separates:

1. current target identification;
2. full-world identification;
3. future certificate protection.

These can recommend different next measurements.

## Scientific meaning

The original EOG idea was not merely “retain many possible worlds.”

It was also to ask what remains reliable as alternative worlds are admitted.

v9 turns that into an experimental-design consequence:

> **The value of evidence depends on the future uncertainty one wants to defend
> against, not only the uncertainty visible in the current survivor set.**

## Boundary

This is a finite known-truth leverage experiment.  The source, barrier and analyst
diagnostics are not assigned real monetary or logistical costs, and the frozen future
growth pool is not claimed to contain every ecologically plausible future model.

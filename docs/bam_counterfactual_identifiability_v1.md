# BAM counterfactual identifiability v1

## Scientific step after the frozen inverse-identifiability paper

The frozen BAM paper establishes that a realised distribution is a many-to-one
projection of A, B and M mechanism states.  Even a complete current occurrence map can
therefore leave a non-singleton survivor fiber.

This successor asks a different question:

> **Does unresolved mechanism identity matter for the ecological forecast or decision
> that is actually being made?**

A non-singleton BAM survivor fiber is not automatically a practical problem.  If every
surviving world predicts the same outcome under a declared counterfactual, the
counterfactual is identified even though the mechanism is not.  Conversely, if
observationally equivalent worlds diverge after a perturbation, the ambiguity is
forecast-relevant.

## Frozen probes

The v1 benchmark uses three idealised mechanism probes:

- release A: remove abiotic limitation, leaving `B & M`;
- release B: remove biotic limitation, leaving `A & M`;
- release M: remove accessibility limitation, leaving `A & B`.

These are exact finite-world thought experiments.  They are not claimed to represent a
literal climate manipulation, interaction removal or corridor intervention.

For each probe two nested targets are evaluated:

1. the exact finite-node counterfactual occupied map;
2. the coarser binary question of whether releasing that constraint expands the current
   occupied support.

The full BAM state is a still finer target.

## Exact evidence burden

The same complete direct evidence library is available for all targets: node-level A,
B, M and movement-arrival state.

For a known truth and current survivor fiber, a measurement is useful when its truth
outcome eliminates at least one survivor whose declared target differs from the truth
target.  Same-target alternative worlds do not need to be eliminated.

Thus the required information can be strictly less than the information required for
full mechanism identification.

The predeclared order is:

`binary decision burden <= exact counterfactual-map burden <= full BAM-state burden`.

Any violation stops scientific interpretation as an implementation/theory failure.

## Prior-art boundary

This project does not claim generic novelty for model discrimination, optimal
experimental design, value of information, set cover, hitting sets, or
decision-focused learning.  Those are established ideas.

The BAM-specific candidate contribution is narrower: start from an
occurrence-conditioned finite BAM survivor fiber and distinguish

- **mechanistically unresolved but counterfactually robust** states;
- **counterfactually consequential ambiguity**;
- the exact evidence burden needed for a declared ecological target rather than for
  full process recovery.

This directly extends the frozen inverse-identifiability result without modifying that
paper.

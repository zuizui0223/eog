# Round-goby real-system BAM bridge v1

## Role

This is the first real-system bridge for the finite BAM target-identifiability programme.

It is **not fresh confirmatory evidence**.  The 2026 paper abstract and aggregate
reported results were already visible before this bridge was designed.

The bridge therefore tests a narrower question:

> Can the finite BAM survivor-fiber machinery be instantiated on a real river invasion
> system and then genuinely falsified by later positive observations without changing
> the world universe?

## Why this system

The Rouge River round-goby invasion gives unusually direct empirical analogues for all
three BAM components:

- A: temperature, baseflow and substrate;
- B: johnny-darter community state as a predeclared biotic proxy;
- M: river distance and time after removal of the Wayne Road Dam.

The restoration event is also biologically meaningful: removing a connectivity barrier
created the route along which the invasive fish subsequently spread.

## Anti-circularity rules

Published regression significance is not used to choose the world universe.

A uses all four declared site habitat variables.  B includes both directions of the
johnny-darter threshold relationship.  M rate levels are derived from site geometry
rather than observed invasion speed.

The temporal split is fixed at 2012–2018 calibration and 2019–2025 heldout.

Only positive focal observations are hard ecological witnesses.  Nondetections remain
observation-process dependent.

## Intended result

The useful endpoint is not whether EOG beats the published regression.

The endpoint is whether real heldout positives:

1. eliminate previously compatible A/B/M worlds;
2. leave a non-singleton survivor fiber;
3. falsify any unanimous ecological reachability conclusion made before those positive
   records were opened.

A negative or null result is retained.

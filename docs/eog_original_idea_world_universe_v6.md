# Original EOG world-universe certificate persistence — v6

## Question

The original EOG idea emphasized conclusions that survive disagreement among many
possible worlds.

v6 tests that directly.

The ecological landscape and the positive occurrence evidence are fixed. Only the
declared candidate-world universe expands:

1. source uncertainty only;
2. source + barrier uncertainty;
3. source + barrier + analyst-rule uncertainty.

## Why 25 percent occurrence coverage

The comparison concerns persistence of both robust-reachable and robust-impossible
certificates.

Complete positive coverage would remove all unobserved truth-reachable nodes from the
comparison and would therefore make the two certificate types incomparable.

Before implementation/scoring, the evidence level was fixed to the already preregistered
v4 25 percent dispersed-farthest-first design.

## The direct test of the original intuition

For every node classified robust-reachable or robust-impossible in the narrow U0
universe, v6 asks whether the certificate survives the full U2 universe.

The predeclared directional hypothesis C4 is intentionally risky:

> robust-impossible certificates persist more often than robust-reachable certificates.

If that asymmetry is absent or reversed, the original intuition is refuted for this
generator and expansion path.

## General invariant

Because U0 is a subset of U1 and U1 a subset of U2, adding worlds cannot manufacture a
new universal statement. A universal certificate can only persist or erode into
contingency.

This monotonicity is audited separately from the empirical C4 comparison.

## Temporal minimum-relaxation frontier — current implementation gate

The static core could already ask which geographic/IBD, environmental/IBE and barrier relaxations are Pareto-minimal among worlds compatible with an observed endpoint configuration. The temporal core could already eliminate worlds that reach a positive occurrence too late. What remained missing was their combination:

> **How does time-stamped positive evidence change the minimum relaxation required to make the observed distribution realizable?**

The temporal relaxation layer therefore keeps relaxation coordinates separate from `TemporalWorld` fingerprints and requires a declaration covering the **complete frozen temporal-world universe**, including worlds later eliminated by observations.

Known-truth target:

- a zero-relaxation slow world reaches endpoint C only by `t3`;
- geographic-only, environmental-only and barrier-only rescue worlds reach C by `t2`;
- a world relaxing all three axes reaches C early but is Pareto dominated;
- with only `C@t3`, the slow baseline is the minimum frontier;
- with the stricter `C@t2` observation, the slow world is incompatible and the frontier becomes the three non-dominated axis-specific rescue explanations;
- no compatible temporal world yields an empty frontier rather than a fabricated rescue.

This is an occurrence-conditioned minimum-required-relaxation diagnostic. It does not claim that a Pareto-minimal world was historically realised, and it does not manufacture a scalar IBD+IBE+barrier water level.

## Stop / next decision

After the temporal minimum-relaxation frontier is validated, do **not** add another operator merely to complete the conceptual picture. The finite architecture already covers compatible-world reconstruction, world-indexed flow sets, static and temporal robust/contingent/excluded structure, basin merge, positive survey discrimination, corridor opening/closure, world-universe monotonicity and minimum-relaxation inference.

The next repository-level decision should be evidence-driven: choose a comparator/validation question that tests whether these outputs add ecological information not already available from simpler or established methods. The strongest unresolved external-method boundary remains that reachability/connectivity alone is heavily covered by existing dynamic occupancy, mechanistic SDM, least-cost, circuit and functional-habitat approaches; the distinctive EOG claim is the inverse, set-valued, occurrence-conditioned constraint/reconstruction layer. ## Stop rule

Do not run another occurrence or genetic dataset merely to obtain a favourable result. Do not retune frozen adverse/null/indeterminate results. Preserve genuinely different worlds as a set whenever observations do not identify one history, and keep claim strength bounded by the explicit coverage certificate.

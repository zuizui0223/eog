## Exact paired design

The 12 landscapes each contribute **64 candidate BAM worlds**, sharing a common two-variable environment and one frozen full-landscape robust median/MAD transform.

For each landscape, hold all but one constraint axis fixed:

1. A narrow vs broad, **384 pairs**.
2. B obligatory partner vs none, **192 pairs**.
3. B antagonist exclusion vs none, **192 pairs**.
4. B partner + antagonist vs none, **192 pairs**.
5. M barrier closed vs open, **384 pairs**.

Only pairs where the restrictive realized `G=A∩B∩M` loses at least one occupied candidate node enter the changed-G directional summaries. Every pair is checked to satisfy `G_restricted ⊆ G_unrestricted`. Identical G gets ratio zero.

For each changed G, compute original shared-reference `span` for G and compare it to the median of **64 deterministic size-matched draws** from the permissive G, using no groupwise rescaling. Complementary mean distance to centroid and original full-G geographic versus environmental-MST component changes remain *different estimands*.

The paired matched log ratio is:

`log(span(restricted G) / median(span(size-matched permissive G samples)))`.

This is not the effect of changing A/B/M in nature, does not estimate the full niche breadth, and is sensitive to the chosen fixed set of environments, pair sizes and reference. Quantiles of the matched draws are sampling sensitivity, not species-level confidence intervals.

| **Total** | **1,344** | **739** | **638** | **62** | **39** | **50** |

The apparent 'increasing span after restriction' phenomenon is **not a universal effect**: in the fixed M barrier closure contrast it was found only in **2 of 12 landscapes** (29 pairs, 17 in S04 and 12 in S06). B antagonist increases occurred in four systems. Further statistical claims must respect the **12 shared landscape units** and the fact that some system parameter contrasts are repeated.

A restriction can reduce the number of occupied nodes while increasing a *particular sampled dispersion summary* relative to a matched subset; it does not make the total set larger. This is different from claims about the latent physiological niche. It is also not evidence that a B or M constraint has *caused* wider niches in wild populations.

The companion GitHub Actions workflow archives full `paired_worlds.csv` and `report.json`. A new receipt should pin the artifact/sha after successful execution; it must be explicitly labeled *post-result replay receipt*. The full result remains separate from the original parent study.

## Next independent evidence

This finite-world result does not identify whether the observed G in any real study was generated by A, B, M, or overlapping constraints. An empirical study must supply separately validated environmental, biotic-partner, spatial-connectivity and observation process information. Any landscape connectivity index derived from contemporary G must be flagged as response-contaminated, rather than used as independent M evidence.

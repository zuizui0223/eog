## Joint uncertainty was not reducible to either axis alone

A strict joint interaction was preregistered as

[

>
max{b(W_1,O_0), b(W_0,O_1)}.
]

This occurred in **51/387** fiber-target rows:

- climate shift: **4/129**;
- biotic stress: **43/129**;

Truth-weighted counts were 10, 270 and 40 respectively.

The biotic-stress result is particularly strong inside this frozen design: every one of
the **43 W1 target-unresolved unique fibers** had a strict joint increment.

This does not mean “uncertainty interacts” universally. It means the exact evidence
required by the combined frozen joint universe could not be read off from either
single-axis expansion alone.

## Example of a strict interaction

A common pattern was:

```text
W0O0   target already identified or needs little evidence
W0O1   assay-process uncertainty alone is manageable
W1O0   ecological expansion alone is manageable
W1O1   an additional assay or calibration becomes necessary
```

For example, one S10 biotic-stress fiber required:

- W0O0: 1 assay;
- W0O1: 2 assays;
- W1O0: 3 assays;
- W1O1: **4 actions**.

The W1O1 exact minimum included assay-process calibration, while a no-calibration design
needed five assays.

Thus the broader ecological family exposed target-discordant alternatives whose assay
codes were ambiguous across the observation worlds.

## Ecological expansion created calibration requirements

In W0O1, none of the exact minima required calibration.

After ecological expansion to W1, explicit calibration became necessary for every
minimum-size design in **9** fiber-target rows:

- climate: **2 fibers**, truth weight 33;
- biotic stress: **4 fibers**, truth weight 44;
- barrier restoration: **3 fibers**, truth weight 36.

This is the main Phase-IX causal-logic statement:

> **Whether measurement-process calibration matters can depend on which ecological
> alternatives are admitted.**

Calibration is not an intrinsic property of an assay alone. It becomes target-relevant
when ecological alternatives turn assay-world ambiguity into target ambiguity.

## But calibration was not the whole interaction

There were **51** strict joint interactions but only **9** new calibration requirements.

So most joint burden increases were handled by additional assay channels rather than
explicit observation-world identification.

This extends the Phase-VII self-calibration result:

> multiple fields can sometimes separate the future target across observation worlds
> without identifying the observation process itself.

## Main interpretation

Previous phases gave separate robustness questions:

- does the decision survive ecological-world expansion?;
- can the assay separate the target across observation worlds?

Phase IX shows that those audits cannot always be treated independently.

The relevant object is the **joint survivor universe**.  Evidence should be designed
against target disagreement in

[
W_E 	imes W_O,
]

because an observation ambiguity can be harmless under one ecological family and become
decisive after that family expands.

error, and the action counts are not field-cost estimates.

# Original EOG evidence-target boundary experiment — v6

## Why v6 exists

v5 showed that directionality and temporal order do not affect the same inferential
object.

Directionality changed both source/history ambiguity and robust impossibility.

Temporal order reduced source/history ambiguity but, at full positive coverage, did not
change robust impossibility.

v6 formalizes that difference.

## Baseline

For every eligible v5 landscape, the baseline is the full-positive directed-static
survivor fiber.

Four additional truth-consistent evidence channels are then evaluated alone and in all
16 subsets:

- T: temporal arrival-depth constraints;
- S: source identity;
- B: barrier-density state;
- R: analyst traversability rule.

## Four evidence effects

A single channel is classified as:

1. redundant — no world removed;
2. identity-only — worlds removed but reachability classes and robust impossibility do
   not change;
3. target-refining — exact reachability classes contract but robust impossibility does
   not change;
4. envelope-changing — robust impossibility strictly grows.

## Target-specific evidence burden

v6 also asks how many channels are needed to identify:

- the truth world's exact reachability set;
- the complete truth source × barrier × analyst world.

The first burden cannot exceed the second under the same evidence library.

This experiment tests a core EOG idea:

> evidence is useful relative to a declared ecological target, and eliminating more
> possible histories is not automatically the same thing as changing what is
> ecologically possible or impossible.

## Exact monotonicity

For fixed evidence (e), target (T), and nested universes

[
W_0\subseteq W_1,
]

the compatible survivor sets satisfy

[
S(e;W_0)\subseteq S(e;W_1).
]

Therefore

[
T(S(e;W_0))
\subseteq
T(S(e;W_1)).
]

The number of target outcomes can stay the same or increase; it cannot decrease merely
because more admissible worlds were added.

Hence target identification is monotone non-increasing under universe expansion.

The observed 64 → 2,592 → complete-closure sequence is one concrete BAM realization of
that exact rule.

## H4 was refuted

The preregistered hypothesis expected finite expansion=True margins to exceed
expansion=False medians in at least two targets.

That pattern did not occur.

Instead the finite structured counterexamples were directionally segregated:

- release A: 6/6 started from **no expansion**;
- release B: 12/12 started from **no expansion**;
- release M: 41/41 started from **expansion**.

This result is retained as observed.

It indicates that robustness direction depends on the actual geometry of the
preregistered ecological operator family.  The generic bit-level witness asymmetry from
Phase III does not automatically determine the direction of fragility under a
restricted ecological neighbourhood.

## Scientific interpretation

The earlier results now form a three-level statement.

1. **Within the original finite BAM family**, many counterfactual decisions are
   identified despite mechanism ambiguity.
2. **Within a preregistered nearby ecological expansion**, most of those decisions
   remain identified.
3. **Within unrestricted same-G decomposition space**, almost all nontrivial decisions
   become unresolved.

So:

> **logical counterexamples may exist very close in A/B/M state space without existing
> inside the first prospectively declared ecological parameter neighbourhood.**

The strength of a BAM decision certificate must therefore include its universe
semantics.

A useful output is no longer just:

`decision = identified`

but:

`decision = identified under W0; survives W1; fails under W2`.

## Boundary

The 2,592-world lattice remains synthetic.  A one-shell partner-range change or one
extra niche-width/dispersal level is not calibrated to any real taxon.

“No counterexample found in W1” means exactly that.  It does not mean no biologically
plausible counterexample exists.

The next empirical/generalization step should therefore calibrate expansion operators
independently of the focal outcome, rather than simply adding more synthetic levels
after seeing this result.

### Negative / conditional results that must stay attached

- **v2 G2 was refuted**, and the environmental autocorrelation association changed sign under alternative analyst worlds; do not promote a universal autocorrelation mechanism. [v2](../eog_original_idea_virtual_worlds_v2_result.md), [v3](../eog_original_idea_analyst_worlds_v3_result.md).
- The v40 predeclared **material** provenance-site advantage was **not met**: fixed confluence vs 64-seed random mean **+0.008560 bit**, below the **+0.020 bit** threshold. Seed wins are not independent ecosystems. [v40](../eog_provenance_placement_multiseed_v40.md).
- The v43 **12/12** maturity readout is **retrospective smoothing**, because its original observation library included observations later than the declared target. Do not report it as a successful forecast; v44 repairs this for a *new* synthetic estimand. [v43](../eog_minimal_temporal_evidence_v43.md), [v44](../eog_original_idea_asof_time_provenance_v44.md).
- **Support topology** has deterministic component/persistence/mask/anchor-lineage synthetic verification, but the original support-only/distance-only **held-out empirical superiority** had not been established by that synthetic verification. [support topology stress](../support_topology_stress_results.md).
- Exact enumeration over **finitely declared** worlds is not a certification for all continuous parameter choices or every plausible real ecosystem. Successful CI and mathematical controls are not independent biological support.

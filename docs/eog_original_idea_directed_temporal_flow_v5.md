# Original EOG directed and temporal flow experiment — v5

## Why v5 exists

v4 showed two things at once:

1. positive occurrences strongly contracted candidate source/barrier/analyst worlds;
2. robust-impossibility recovery stayed exactly flat as more positives were added.

That plateau occurred in static undirected connectivity worlds.

v5 restores the flow ideas that were central to the original EOG concept:

- direction;
- upstream/downstream asymmetry;
- first-arrival order.

## Flow worlds

Each frozen v2 latent landscape receives one response-independent outlet corner.

Edges are oriented from larger to smaller squared distance from that outlet after the
same A/B, geographic, environmental and barrier filters are applied.

The truth source is the most upstream A/B-permissive node in the largest geographic
component.

Candidate source identity remains unknown.

## Three inference regimes

The same source × barrier × analyst candidate family is evaluated as:

1. undirected static;
2. directed static;
3. directed plus reached-by-depth temporal positive evidence.

This isolates what flow direction and occurrence order add beyond the v4 static cloud.

## Primary question

Does directed/temporal flow transform extra occurrence records from merely eliminating
world labels into genuinely new information about:

- source identity;
- robust impossibility;
- asymmetric occurrence relations?

A failure to break the v4 plateau remains a valid result.

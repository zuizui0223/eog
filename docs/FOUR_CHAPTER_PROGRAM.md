# Four-chapter research program

Program ID: `niche-to-survey-four-chapter-v1`

The chapter order is fixed:

1. **SDMR — environmental niche-driver selection**: which environmental dimensions best define an interpretable realized niche, with model-pool occurrences used for choice and sealed occurrences reserved for answer-checking.
2. **ODSP — multidimensional niche geometry**: how much niche structure is hidden by projecting support onto x-y; quantify added vertical, temporal and structural state-space information.
3. **EOG — distributional worlds and reachability**: given supported local states and declared transitions/barriers, which distributional worlds remain compatible with evidence and what states are possible, robust, unresolved or reachable.
4. **ACSP — survey action**: where field effort should be directed next; return bounded candidate survey patches/priorities.

## Chapter 3 scientific center

EOG is the **distributional possibility** chapter. It begins after local support/possibility has been declared by an upstream representation. That representation may come from SDMR, ODSP-aware support, another SDM, environmental similarity, telemetry-derived support or an expert-declared field.

EOG does not decide which environmental variables define the niche and does not measure niche thickness. Instead it asks:

> **Given local support, current positive evidence and a prospectively declared finite universe of transition/process worlds, which worlds remain compatible, and what future/unsampled states survive world disagreement?**

Primary objects remain:

- exact compatible-world identities for sequential update/falsification;
- per-world reachability/support;
- possible, robust and unresolved projections;
- contraction or finite-universe falsification after new evidence;
- label-invariant predictive summaries for the active EOG-WF interface.

### Fixed Chapter-3 goal

Establish a prospectively validated framework in which uncertainty about dispersal, transition and barrier structure is retained as a world set rather than collapsed to one guessed historical process, and test whether the surviving-world representation adds reproducible predictive information on heterogeneous fresh endpoints.

### Current boundary

The active paper mainline and endpoint-3 hard stop remain unchanged. This chapter map does not authorize new connectivity operators, retrospective retuning, or a fourth endpoint. Chapter 3 closes when the already-frozen third valid heterogeneous endpoint reaches its terminal decision and the paper package is completed.

## Interface to the other chapters

- Chapter 1 may supply an interpretable environmental support representation.
- Chapter 2 may reveal that a planar support field discards time/vertical state structure; EOG may consume an axis-resolved support state in a future separately validated programme.
- Chapter 4 may use EOG outputs when the decision problem is where to survey, but EOG's own hypothesis-discriminating survey score remains distinct from ACSP's occurrence/support-oriented candidate-patch product.

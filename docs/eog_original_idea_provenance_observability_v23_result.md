## Exact history identifiability by evidence class

## Genuine complementarity

The two evidence classes disagreed strongly about which histories they could resolve.

- occupancy identified but provenance did not: **397** rows;
- provenance identified but occupancy did not: **90** rows.

So provenance is not merely a weaker or stronger version of temporal occupancy.

It exposes a different coordinate of the history.

## Provenance rescues v22 failures

v22 left exact activation history unresolved in 189 rows.

Provenance-only rescued **90** of those.

The combined occupancy + provenance library rescued **108**.

This directly confirms that some history aliases surviving complete spatial snapshots
are separated by source-of-first-arrival information.

## But 81 exact histories remain unresolved

Even after every frozen occupancy snapshot and every equilibrium provenance tag were
available, exact activation history remained unresolved in **81/768** design rows.

That residual is important.

The next step should not automatically add a new measurement type.

First ask whether those exact-history aliases differ on any ecological target that
matters.

If they are equivalent for every declared consequence, exact history recovery is an
unnecessary target.

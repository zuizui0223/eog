# BAM future-target evidence design v1

## Question

Phase V showed that present BAM-state identity can be insufficient for a future
counterfactual target when latent parameter aliases are dormant under present
conditions but reactivated by the transformation.

This successor asks:

> **What should be measured now if the inferential target is a future ecological
> decision rather than the complete present mechanism?**

## Two evidence libraries

### Present-state evidence

Exact node-level measurements of:

- A;
- B;
- M accessibility;
- movement first-arrival state.

These measurements can identify the complete current BAM state but, by construction,
cannot distinguish parameter worlds that share that current state.

### Parameter evidence

Exact assays of the eight frozen W1 coordinates:

- A niche-breadth level;
- partner requirement;
- antagonist exclusion;
- partner range;
- antagonist range;
- dispersal-radius level;
- barrier permeability;
- movement horizon.

These are synthetic exact assays in the known-truth benchmark. A unit assay count is a
combinatorial information count, not a field-cost model.

## Target-specific design

For truth (w_*), same-G survivor fiber (S), and future target (T), nuisance worlds
are only

[
N_T=\{w\in S:T(w)\neq T(w_*)\}.
]

The evidence design need eliminate (N_T), not every alternative world.

Thus a future decision can require less information than exact parameter-world
identification.

Conversely, if two worlds have identical present A/B/M/tau but different future
targets, no amount of present-state measurement can separate them.  A parameter-sensitive
assay or another transformation-sensitive observation is necessary.

## Frozen transformations

The evidence audit reuses without modification:

- climate shift;
- biotic stress;
- barrier restoration;

and the W1 2,592-world ecological lattice from Phases IV–V.

No new transformation or evidence channel may be added after scoring.

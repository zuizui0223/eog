# Known-truth BAM evidence ladder — v2.1 to v2.3 synthesis

## Question

For a virtual focal species whose realised distribution is generated by

[
G = A \cap B \cap M,
]

what evidence is required to identify the underlying finite-landscape BAM state?

- **A** = abiotic viability
- **B** = explicit biotic permissibility from independently generated required-partner and antagonist distributions
- **M** = source-conditioned accessibility under distance, hard barrier and finite horizon

The candidate universe contains 64 BAM worlds. A, B and M remain separate state objects; no weighted suitability score is used.

## v2.1 — orthogonal BAM activation

All preregistered activation gates passed before scoring:

- partner occupancy: 15/21
- antagonist occupancy: 9/21
- A-only witness pairs: 16
- partner-B-only witness pairs: 11
- antagonist-B-only witness pairs: 6
- M-distance-only witness pairs: 7
- M-barrier-only witness pairs: 12
- same-final-G / different-arrival pairs: 812
- positive-superset / diagnostic-negative pairs: 1,097

Thus A, B and M were all independently capable of generating falsifying contrasts.

### Evidence through v2.1

| Evidence | BAM truth uniquely identified |
|---|---:|
| complete positive occurrences | 0/64 = 0% |
| + perfect surveyed negatives | 2/64 = 3.125% |
| + exact arrival time on occupied nodes | 6/64 = 9.375% |

Perfect negatives eliminated 1,097/1,097 positive-superset false worlds.

Occupied-node timing eliminated 812/812 equal-G alternatives whose M arrival predictions differed on the realised occupied set.

Yet 58/64 BAM truths remained non-identifiable.

## v2.2 — direct A and B evidence

The same frozen 64-world universe was retained.

The primary endpoint changed from exact parameter labels to exact **BAM-state identity**:

[
(A,B,M,\text{arrival state}).
]

Parameter tuples that induce the same finite-landscape BAM state are aliases, not distinct biological mechanisms.

There were 48 distinct BAM states among the 64 parameter worlds.

### Evidence ladder

| Evidence level | BAM state uniquely identified |
|---|---:|
| E0: complete positive occurrences | 0/64 = 0% |
| E1: + complete perfect negatives | 2/64 = 3.125% |
| E2: + occupied-node arrival timing | 6/64 = 9.375% |
| E3: + complete direct A state | 8/64 = 12.5% |
| E4: + complete direct B state | 21/64 = 32.8125% |

The hypothesis that complete G + M timing + complete A + complete B would recover the BAM state was **refuted**.

Residual non-identifiability after E4: **43/64**.

A deterministic targeted design reproduced the full A/B contraction with:

- direct A: 0 or 1 diagnostic node;
- direct B: 0, 1 or 2 diagnostic nodes;
- total direct A+B measurements: at most 3 nodes.

Therefore the remaining ambiguity was not caused by insufficient A/B measurement. It was movement state outside realised occupancy.

## v2.3 — direct M evidence

Independent source-conditioned movement evidence was then added.

### E5: complete direct M accessibility

BAM-state unique fraction increased from

[
21/64 = 32.8125\%
]

to

[
64/64 = 100\%.
]

No additional full-arrival evidence was required for BAM-state recovery in this finite universe:

### E6: complete M arrival state

[
64/64 = 100\%.
]

Exact parameter-label recovery remained 50%, because 16 parameter aliases occur in groups that induce exactly the same BAM state.

That ambiguity is retained deliberately.

## Targeted movement measurements

Complete M did not require measuring all 21 nodes.

The full E5 contraction was reproduced with:

- 0 direct-M nodes in 21/64 truths;
- 1 direct-M node in 36/64 truths;
- 2 direct-M nodes in 7/64 truths.

No additional arrival-time node was required after the targeted M-accessibility contraction.

Most frequently diagnostic movement locations were:

- r0c4: 20 truths
- r0c3: 18 truths
- r0c6: 12 truths

These are locations where alternative movement worlds disagree but realised occupancy alone does not expose that disagreement.

## Main scientific result

The simulation does **not** show that occurrence data recover the process that generated a distribution.

It shows the opposite:

> **A realised distribution is a lossy intersection of abiotic, biotic and movement constraints. Even complete presence/absence and colonisation timing can leave the generating BAM decomposition non-identifiable because constraints outside the realised occupied set remain hidden.**

EOG's useful object is therefore the surviving equivalence class of BAM worlds, not a forced best mechanism.

The evidence ladder also shows how the ambiguity can be broken:

1. positive occurrences eliminate worlds with positive witnesses;
2. surveyed negatives eliminate overly permissive worlds;
3. temporal evidence eliminates movement worlds with incompatible arrival histories;
4. direct A/B measurements remove latent suitability and interaction ambiguity;
5. targeted direct-M accessibility measurements reveal movement constraints hidden outside realised occupancy.

## Current boundary

This result is exact only for the declared finite virtual landscape and 64-world BAM universe.

The strongest next test is **cross-landscape generality**:

> Does the same evidence ordering and occurrence-only non-identifiability recur across independently generated environmental geometry, biotic configurations and movement barriers?

That is the v3 target. No claim of universal measurement counts (for example “two M nodes are always enough”) is authorized from v2.3.

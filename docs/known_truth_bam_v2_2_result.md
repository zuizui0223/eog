# Known-truth BAM v2.2 — result interpretation

## Why this is the first full BAM benchmark in this line

Earlier simulation stages exposed two design failures before a full BAM claim was allowed:

- v2: required partner occupied the entire landscape and A produced no independent witness;
- v2.1: A/B/M axes were independently activated, but the required partner did not occupy the focal source, so obligate-partner worlds could not serve as truth.

v2.2 fixes both problems before scoring. The activation audit passed:

- partner occupancy = **9/21 = 42.9%**;
- antagonist occupancy = **12/21 = 57.1%**;
- focal source lies inside the partner distribution;
- all four B masks are distinct;
- all **40/40** candidate BAM worlds are eligible as truth;
- A-only witness = **3**;
- partner-B-only witness = **12**;
- antagonist-B-only witness = **12**;
- movement-distance-only witness = **5**;
- movement-barrier-only witness = **1**;
- same-final-G / different-arrival-time pairs = **82**.

Thus A, positive B, negative B, dispersal distance, barrier permeability and movement timing are all independently exercised.

## Main result

All preregistered logical hypotheses H1-H8 were supported within the frozen v2.2 universe.

But the important result is not that EOG recovered the true BAM world.

It usually did not.

### Positive occurrences only

Unique true BAM world:

**0/40 = 0%**

Positive observations can eliminate a candidate only when at least one observed occupied node lies outside that candidate's A, B or M set.

A more permissive candidate whose realised set contains the truth cannot be rejected by positive observations, regardless of how completely the true occupied set is sampled.

### Add complete perfect surveyed negatives

Unique true BAM world:

**3/40 = 7.5%**

Under the explicit perfect-detection/equilibrium contract, negatives eliminated every diagnostic positive-superset alternative:

**negative rescue fraction = 1.0**

This demonstrates that negative evidence supplies a qualitatively different witness from positive occurrences.

However, even exact knowledge of the final occupied/unoccupied pattern usually failed to identify the BAM decomposition because different A/B/M mechanisms can generate the same final G.

### Add temporal colonisation evidence

Unique true BAM world:

**8/40 = 20%**

Among equal-G worlds with different movement arrival times, temporal evidence eliminated every diagnostic alternative:

**temporal M rescue fraction = 1.0**

Yet 80% of full BAM truth states remained non-unique.

Therefore even exact final occupancy plus correct movement timing does not generally identify A, B and M separately.

## Biotic interaction result

The positive-interaction result is especially informative.

For **7 diagnostic obligate-partner truth cases**:

- the otherwise identical no-B world survived positive-only evidence in **7/7**;
- perfect surveyed negatives eliminated that no-B world in **7/7**.

Thus presence co-occurring with a required partner cannot by itself establish that the partner is required, because the no-interaction world is more permissive and also accommodates every positive occurrence.

The same asymmetry appeared for antagonist exclusion.

For **10 diagnostic antagonist-exclusion truth cases**:

- the no-B world survived positive-only evidence in **10/10**;
- perfect surveyed negatives eliminated it in **10/10**.

This is an evidence-asymmetry result, not a statement that negatives are automatically trustworthy in empirical data. The negative rescue depends on the explicitly idealized perfect-detection/equilibrium contract.

## BAM interpretation

The occupied distribution is

[
G_0 = A cap B cap M.
]

Occurrence evidence observes G0, not A, B and M separately.

Consequently the inverse problem is many-to-one:

[
(A,B,M) longrightarrow G_0
]

may map many distinct BAM triples onto the same realised distribution.

The simulations show three levels of information:

1. **Positive occurrence** can falsify under-permissive candidate worlds when a positive witness exists.
2. **Perfect surveyed negatives** can falsify over-permissive supersets under a strong observation contract.
3. **Temporal colonisation evidence** can resolve some M ambiguity hidden by the final distribution.

But none of these guarantees recovery of the complete causal BAM decomposition.

## Strongest defensible claim

> **Occurrence data constrain BAM mechanisms through witnesses, but even complete spatial occupancy and movement timing need not identify the Abiotic, Biotic and Movement processes that generated a distribution. EOG is therefore better framed as a mechanism-falsification and identifiability framework than as a true-process selector.**

## What this changes for EOG

The natural output is no longer one selected process.

For each candidate explanation EOG should report:

- which A/B/M constraints have positive witnesses against them;
- which more-permissive alternatives survive because positive evidence cannot reject them;
- which alternatives are eliminated only after a declared negative-evidence contract;
- which M alternatives are eliminated only by temporal evidence;
- which BAM decompositions remain observationally equivalent.

The remaining set of worlds is itself the scientific result.

## Next evidence layer

The next scientifically meaningful simulation is not a larger random sweep.

It is to ask which independent evidence breaks the residual BAM equivalence classes:

- imperfect-detection negatives;
- interaction intervention/removal;
- independent partner/antagonist abundance observations;
- genetics or ancestry;
- repeated colonisation/extinction time series.

Each should be added under a separate frozen observation contract, because different evidence types answer different parts of the BAM inverse problem.

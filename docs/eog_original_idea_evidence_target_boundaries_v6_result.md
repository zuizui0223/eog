## Target burden versus full-world burden

Among 272 eligible directed-flow worlds, only 17 already had the exact truth
reachability class identified from complete positive occurrence evidence.

The minimum additional channel burden for exact reachability-target identification was:

- 0 channels: 17 rows;
- 1: 106;

For full source × barrier × analyst-world identification:

- 0 channels: 8;

Target burden never exceeded world burden.

In **97/272** rows, the ecological reachability target could be identified with strictly
less information than the full generating world.

## Temporal evidence was not envelope-changing

Temporal arrival-depth evidence:

- redundant: 202 rows;
- identity-only: 53;
- target-refining: 17;
- envelope-changing: **0**.

There were **70** rows where temporal evidence removed worlds but added zero new robust
impossibility.

This confirms the v5 D5 refutation at a more exact level: arrival order can be highly
informative about source/history identity without changing what nodes remain possible
under the surviving world set.

## Barrier and analyst evidence often crossed the ecological target boundary

Barrier-state evidence was:

- envelope-changing in **133/272** rows;
- identity-only in 53;
- target-refining in 12;
- redundant in 74.

Analyst-rule evidence was:

- envelope-changing in **115/272**;
- target-refining in 26;
- identity-only in 19;
- redundant in 112.

Thus uncertainty about physical barriers and uncertainty about the analytical
environment-to-traversability rule often affected the ecological possibility envelope
itself.

## Source evidence usually refined the target without changing robust impossibility

Source identity was:

- target-refining in **182/272** rows;
- envelope-changing in 19;
- identity-only in 18;
- redundant in 53.

This distinguishes exact reachability-class information from robust-exclusion
information. Knowing the source often tells us which exact reachability pattern is the
truth target, but only sometimes creates new universally excluded nodes.

## Four kinds of evidence effect all occurred

Across the frozen panel all four categories existed:

1. redundant;
2. identity-only;
3. target-refining;
4. envelope-changing.

Therefore an evidence channel cannot be described simply as “informative” or
“uninformative.”

The relevant question is:

> **Which equivalence boundary does the evidence cross for the ecological target we
> actually care about?**

## Current EOG interpretation

The virtual-world programme now separates at least four inferential quantities:

- world/history identity;
- source identity;
- exact reachability target class;
- robust possibility/impossibility envelope.

Different evidence channels act on different layers.

That gives a principled next step: active evidence selection should choose the next
observation by the target boundary it is expected to cross, rather than by generic
world-count reduction.

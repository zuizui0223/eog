# Original EOG analyst-world sensitivity — v3 result

## Result

All five preregistered analyst-world hypotheses were supported.

The main result is not that one environmental rule was best.  It is that the same
latent ecological landscape can yield qualitatively different reachability conclusions
depending on how the analyst translates environmental difference into traversability.

## Analyst disagreement

Across 384 frozen landscapes, the mean fraction of locally permissive non-source
targets whose reachability depended on the analyst rule was **0.366**.

There were:

- 4,143 contingent target classifications;
- 3,777 targets reachable under all three rules;
- 3,414 targets unreachable under all three rules.

So analyst uncertainty did not make everything unresolved.  Robust and contingent
relations coexisted.

## Environmental autocorrelation changed sign

The frozen v2 q70 rule had produced the surprising result that high-autocorrelation
landscapes had more endpoint-similar/pathwise-blocked pairs.

v3 retained that result for the same q70 rule:

- q70 low minus high: **-0.180**.

But the other two predeclared analyst worlds reversed the sign:

- absolute raw tolerance 0.5: **+0.634**;
- standardized 1 SD tolerance: **+0.213**.

Thus the qualitative conclusion about environmental autocorrelation was not a robust
property of the latent landscape generator.  It depended on the analyst's rule for
turning environmental differences into edge traversability.

This does not rescue v2 G2.  G2 remains refuted under its frozen rule.

## Barrier effect survived analyst uncertainty

The high-minus-low barrier-density contrast in viable-but-unreachable nodes remained
positive for every analyst rule:

- q70: **+0.325**;
- absolute 0.5: **+0.155**;
- standardized 1 SD: **+0.256**.

This is exactly the distinction EOG wanted to make:

> some conclusions are analyst-contingent, while others survive disagreement among
> plausible response-independent analytical worlds.

## Universe expansion behaved monotonically

Adding analyst worlds:

- gained 1,423 possible target classifications relative to q70 alone;
- removed 1,423 q70-only robust-impossibility certificates;
- removed 2,720 q70-only robust-reachability certificates;
- created **0** monotonicity violations.

So adding analyst possibilities weakened certainty rather than manufacturing stronger
impossibility.

## Scientific meaning

The v1 canonical archetypes showed that the original EOG concepts can be separated.
The v2 randomized panel showed that most of them remain common under a less curated
generator and also produced one real refutation.
v3 now shows why a set-valued world approach matters even when the ecological
landscape is held fixed:

> **the mapping from landscape to reachability is itself an uncertain analytical
> object.**

The active EOG virtual-world programme should therefore keep two uncertainty axes
separate:

1. ecological/natural world;
2. analyst/representation world.

A conclusion deserves a stronger robustness label only when it survives both.

# Portal DM fresh paired-complementarity attempt

This directory begins one fresh paired test of the unchanged EOG Layer-B summary in
the Portal Project's 24 fenced Chihuahuan-desert plots. The focal endpoint is monthly
observed *Dipodomys merriami* capture reappearance, not latent occupancy or individual
movement.

The pinned source physically separates the 7x7 stake coordinates, plot treatments,
trapping-effort ledger, new-moon registry and species registry from the row-level rodent
capture file. Stage one may open only those response-independent files and exactly one
bounded physical header record from the response. It cannot download the response
payload, fit a model, score a heldout year or authorize outcome access.

The analysis registry is all 24 plot IDs. Each plot point is fixed as the arithmetic
mean of its 49 released trapping-stake coordinate rows. The pinned coordinate file has
one response-independent label anomaly: plot 1 omits stake number 54 and labels two
distinct released coordinates as 55. Both rows are retained exactly as released; no
label is repaired and no coordinate is imputed. Three response-blind structural coverage
levels are requested at largest-component fractions 0.25, 0.50 and 0.75, plus a
full-support conditional world. These are analyst-choice structural regimes, not claims
about biological dispersal limits.

Calibration targets end in 2011. Calendar years 2012 through 2019 are the eight heldout
outer units. A plot-period zero is eligible only when the separate effort ledger says
`sampled=1`, `effort>=47` and `qcflag=1`, and transitions must join consecutive new-moon
indices. The predictive claim is explicitly conditional on currently recorded internal
plot sources; outside immigration is not represented or interpreted.

If stage one is green, the exact observed physical header and derived geometry evidence
will be frozen with the complete 16-key outcome-access ledger, identical strong learners,
the unchanged `symmetric_world_support_summary_v1`, exact count minima and a synthetic
full-path smoke test. Only a later marker-only commit may authorize one response opening.

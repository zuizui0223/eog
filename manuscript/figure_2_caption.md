**Figure 2. Frozen A-Islands confirmatory structural benchmark.** (A) The analysis used 842 islands, 886 declared native taxa, 5 spatial folds, 5 CHELSA predictors and 12 predeclared graph scenarios. (B) Held-out occupied and unoccupied islands were compared only within the same frozen 5 × 5 pointwise-support × nearest-training-occurrence-distance stratum, so the structural score asks whether configuration retains incidence information after those two simpler quantities are controlled. (C) All 845 taxa with a primary species statistic are shown: 672 were above the 0.5 concordance null, 42 equalled it and 131 were below it; 41 declared taxa were not estimable. (D) Mean species-level conditional concordance was 0.618 (95% bootstrap interval 0.609–0.627) for combined connected frequency, 0.615 (0.606–0.624) for geography-only connected frequency, 0.606 (0.597–0.615) for environmentally constrained connected frequency, and 0.529 (0.518–0.540; n=793) for the predeclared normalized geographic bottleneck secondary. The bottleneck evaluation uses the negative median normalized bottleneck, so higher concordance means that smaller bottlenecks align with occupancy. These mode estimates are descriptive; no pairwise superiority test among the modes was predeclared. (E) Non-estimable species-folds remain visible: the primary analysis had 3,041 evaluable rows, 1,190 rows without comparable presence–absence pairs in the frozen strata and 199 rows with insufficient training classes; the bottleneck secondary had 2,591, 1,640 and 199, respectively. Connected frequency and bottleneck are structural graph summaries, not dispersal or colonisation probabilities or realised pathways.

# Figure 4 accessibility description

Three stacked rows show how the EOG conclusion changes with the declared reference and endpoint. The first row is the original A-Islands conditional-concordance test, whose estimate 0.618 and interval 0.609 to 0.627 lie above the 0.5 null. The second row is the prospective A-Islands strong-reference predictive test. Its C-minus-R3 log-loss estimate is +0.0035, interval +0.0025 to +0.0045; positive values mean worse prediction and the result is adverse. The third row is the Tanzania primary LOSO strong-reference test, with log-loss difference +0.032, interval +0.017 to +0.049; its spatial-block sensitivity is described as uncertain rather than confirming a universal adverse effect. All rows use separate scales because conditional concordance and predictive-loss differences are incompatible metrics.

# Figure and table captions — paper-ready EOG-WF manuscript

## Figure 1. Two-layer EOG architecture

**Environmental Occupancy Geometry separates exact structural inference from predictive compression.** Occurrence evidence updates **Layer A**, which retains exact identities of the declared accessibility/continuity worlds that remain compatible and records world exclusion or finite-universe falsification. The surviving ensemble is then mapped to the unchanged ten-feature, world-label-invariant **Layer B** representation `symmetric_world_support_summary_v1`. Predictive complementarity is tested with a matched comparison in which the same strong conventional learner is fitted with and without Layer B. The observed three-endpoint programme fixes the supported boundary at `structural_diagnostic_plus_context_dependent_predictive_complement`; the architecture does not assert that a surviving Layer-A world is the true movement history.

**Source asset:** `figure_1_two_layer_architecture.svg`.

## Figure 2. Prospective validation funnel

**Fresh predictive endpoints were admitted through prospective data-contract gates rather than selected by favorable performance.** The final ledger contains 37 handled records: 34 scientific denominator units and three administrative exclusions. The scientific denominator consists of three valid scored predictive endpoints and 31 source/registry/geometry/effort/schema/estimability STOPs. STOPs document protocol integrity and are not adverse Layer-B results. The three scored endpoints terminate as two favorable and one adverse result. Candidate hunting was hard-stopped after the valid third predictive endpoint.

**Source asset:** `figure_2_candidate_funnel.svg`; full row-level projection: `candidate_flow_table.csv`.

## Figure 3. Endpoint-wise paired predictive performance

**Incremental predictive value of the fixed Layer-B summary is heterogeneous across three fresh ecological systems.** Delta is augmented macro log loss minus baseline macro log loss, so negative values favor Layer B and positive values favor the conventional reference learner. Azores yellow eel telemetry: delta = -0.0099856, 5/5 augmented held-out wins. Southwest Louisiana King Rail passive acoustics: delta = -0.0009718, 7/8 augmented wins. Tampa Bay seagrass monitoring: delta = +0.1010287, 1/5 augmented wins. Endpoints are displayed separately under the preregistered synthesis; observations are not pooled across ecosystems and no common-effect estimate is inferred.

**Source asset:** `figure_3_endpoint_performance.svg`; numeric source: `fresh_endpoint_results.csv`.

## Figure 4. Layer-A / Layer-B decoupling in Southwest Louisiana King Rail

**Structural falsification and predictive complementarity are different estimands.** During the Louisiana endpoint, sequential evidence eventually falsified all six frozen local Layer-A worlds and only the permissive `external_open` world survived. Nevertheless, the unchanged Layer-B summary produced a small favorable paired result: baseline macro log loss 0.2463173, augmented macro log loss 0.2453455, delta = -0.0009718, with augmented loss lower in 7/8 held-out occasions. The predictive gain therefore cannot be interpreted as confirmation of any frozen local world or as identification of a realized movement mechanism.

**Source asset:** `figure_4_louisiana_decoupling.svg`.

The paper presents Environmental Occupancy Geometry (EOG), a two-layer ecological inference framework designed to keep two questions separate. **Layer A** retains the exact identities of a finite set of declared accessibility or continuity worlds and records their sequential compatibility and falsification. **Layer B** compresses the surviving world ensemble into a fixed ten-feature representation that is invariant to arbitrary world labels. We then ask a deliberately narrow predictive question: does this frozen structural summary add held-out information beyond the same strong conventional learner?

We believe this work fits *Methods in Ecology and Evolution* because the contribution is methodological and reproducibility-focused rather than a claim for one universal ecological mechanism. Accessibility, connectivity, dynamic occupancy and dispersal modelling are established fields; EOG does not claim priority for them. Its distinction is the combination of exact world-set auditing, label-invariant predictive compression, matched held-out comparison, prospective response-access controls, explicit favorable/null/adverse/STOP semantics and a prohibition on post-outcome world redesign.

The completed prospective programme is intentionally heterogeneous. The augmented EOG arm was favorable for **Azores yellow eel acoustic telemetry** (macro log loss 0.142273 to 0.132287; 5/5 held-out wins) and **Southwest Louisiana King Rail passive acoustics** (0.246317 to 0.245346; 7/8 wins). The valid third endpoint, **Tampa Bay seagrass transect monitoring**, was adverse: log loss increased from 0.337735 to 0.438764 and the augmented arm won only 1/5 folds. A predeclared secondary ten-feature placebo did not rescue that result. The adverse endpoint is retained in full and prospectively fixes the method boundary at **structural diagnostic plus context-dependent predictive complementarity**.

The paper also reports the complete prospective validation funnel: **31 scientific/protocol STOPs** and three administrative exclusions in addition to the three scored endpoints. These STOPs are not treated as negative biological results. They document where source transport, registries, geometry, effort, surveyed-negative semantics, physical linkage or frozen schema requirements could not be satisfied without prohibited repair. We see this execution record as part of the methodological contribution because it makes candidate selection and outcome access auditable.

All manuscript-facing result tables and figures are generated deterministically from frozen repository certificates and are regression-tested against the committed copies. No additional favorable endpoint will be sought: candidate hunting was hard-stopped after the first valid third predictive terminal. The software, validation contracts, terminal certificates and manuscript projections are public; a final archived release DOI and immutable release tag will be inserted before submission.

The manuscript does **not** claim universal predictive superiority, causal identification, recovery of a true dispersal history or truth of an exact Layer-A world. Instead, it provides a reproducible way to preserve structural impossibility and falsification while requiring any predictive augmentation to demonstrate its value prospectively.

## Current empirical statements

| System | Supported statement |
|---|---|

## Cross-system synthesis allowed now

The sign of structural added value is context dependent.

Two design properties are plausible moderators:
1. whether the reference already represents the same structural information;
2. whether the structural representation is refreshed at the response-relevant scale.

These are hypotheses, not identified causes.

## Prospective claim to earn

A future response-blind cross-system test may support:

> Structural information adds prediction chiefly when it is both nonredundant with the reference and aligned with the response-generating context.

Until that test closes, this sentence is a prediction, not a result.

# Figure 3 caption

**Leakage-safe Tanzania current-flow versus EOG benchmark.** For each species and outer fold, current-flow resistance was selected using outer-training responses only; nearest-occurrence and EOG features were constructed from training occurrences, the selected current-flow quantity was reused in both probability tiers, and the untouched held-out label was scored. The strict reference contained patch area, selected matrix-aware current flow, their interaction, and nearest-training-occurrence distance; the candidate added EOG connected frequency. Differences are candidate minus reference, so negative values favour EOG and positive values are worse. Panel C displays all 60 species under primary leave-one-fragment-out validation: 17 species had negative and 43 had positive mean differences. The species-macro primary LOSO difference was +0.032 (95% interval +0.017 to +0.049; 826 matched rows), showing an adverse incremental result. The primary spatial-block sensitivity was smaller and uncertain (+0.011, 95% interval -0.012 to +0.033; 718 matched rows). Non-estimable rows and their single-class or current-flow-selection reasons are retained explicitly.

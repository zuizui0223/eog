# Inferential-openness paper — claim boundary v1

## Supported

The EOG candidate funnel is a finite prospective workflow denominator.

Within that denominator:
- 3 scientific candidates reached scored predictive endpoints;
- 31 terminated at scientific/protocol STOPs;
- 3 administrative records are excluded from the scientific denominator.

The 31 STOPs can be summarized into four operational layers without changing their original terminal labels:
- transport/source identity: 11;
- registry/geometry/time: 10;
- source separation/linkage/schema: 8;
- semantic/covariate validity: 2.

The 284b Level-C v3/v5 route independently demonstrates a later inferential barrier:
- 12 candidate systems screened;
- 2 architecture-qualified;
- 0 candidate-specific negative-state calibration passes;
- 0 focal hard dependency endpoints opened.

## Strongest synthesis

> Public availability and inferential readiness are different properties. A claim-ready ecological dataset requires a reproducible chain from source identity through registry and response semantics to calibration of the biological state used by the endpoint.

## Do not claim

- a field-wide failure rate;
- random sampling of public datasets;
- that every EOG STOP was caused by the data producer rather than by the prospectively strict workflow contract;
- that temporary transport failures imply permanent unavailability;
- that response-blind schema stops are biological negatives;
- that the 284b retained candidate relations are false;
- that candidate-specific calibration failure proves low sensitivity.

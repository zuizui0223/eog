# Inferential openness — Figure legends v1

These legends use only the prospectively frozen EOG candidate-flow table and the separately frozen 284b cross-project evidence ledger. Counts describe these workflows, not a random sample of ecological data.

## Figure 1. From open resources to claim-ready evidence

Five distinct requirements for using public ecological records to support or contradict a particular scientific claim: exact source/version retrieval, response-independent opportunity registration, unambiguous linkage, admissible observation semantics, and observation-process calibration. The ladder is a conceptual synthesis; an unmet condition prevents the declared inference but does not establish that the underlying ecological state is absent.

## Figure 2. Prospective candidate funnel and terminal barriers

Of 34 scientific candidate attempts, three reached scored predictive endpoints and 31 terminated with preregistered scientific/protocol STOPs. The 31 STOPs are displayed by operational layer: transport/source identity (11), registry/geometry/time (10), separation/linkage/schema (8), and semantic/covariate validity (2). Three administrative exclusions are outside the scientific denominator. The percentages refer to this declared workflow alone.

## Figure 3. Response access at terminal STOP

Among 31 scientific/protocol STOPs, 29 occurred without any biological-response access, one followed header-only access, and one followed a single full-response opening before a terminal schema/linkage failure. Accordingly, 30 of 31 STOPs occurred before biological-response row values had been opened. None of these terminal STOPs was scored as an adverse biological result.

## Figure 4. Negative-state calibration as a later inferential barrier

The independent biological-relation programme screened 12 candidate systems, retained two with identifiable relation/event/function source architecture, and found that neither had independent candidate-specific calibration sufficient to identify an absent function from nondetection. Thus zero hard-negative dependency endpoints were authorized for opening. These outcomes are unresolved, not evidence that either proposed dependency is false.

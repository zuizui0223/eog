Positioning rule:
These references establish prior art for accessibility-aware distribution modelling,
history matching/model-set elimination, multiple working hypotheses, and observation
design for model discrimination. The manuscript does not claim novelty for those generic
ideas. Its contribution is the EOG-specific separation of response-blind structural
testability from response-conditioned world survival, the prospective empirical
falsification, and the explicit identifiability boundary under the declared observation
rule.

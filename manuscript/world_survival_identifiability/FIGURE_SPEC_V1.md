## Figure 3 — Identifiability witness

Use one fixed nonempty, noncomplete undirected graph drawn twice.

Left:
Choose two adjacent nodes as positives.
The induced positive subgraph contains one edge.
World survives.

Right:
Choose two non-adjacent nodes as positives.
The induced positive subgraph contains two isolated nodes.
World fails.

Everything else is visually identical.

Caption sentence:
"The global graph and positive-set cardinality are unchanged; only positive-node placement
changes, so graph structure alone cannot universally determine one-step world survival."

This is a proof illustration, not a claim of graph-theory novelty.

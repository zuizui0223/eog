## Citation boundary

The manuscript may use these references to delimit method roles, but should not infer the following from citation alone:

- that pointwise SDMs universally ignore space or dispersal;
- that barrier SPDE, dynamic occupancy, least-cost paths, circuit theory, habitat networks and EOG estimate the same quantity;
- that connected frequency is a colonisation or dispersal probability;
- that multidimensional island isolation, stepping stones, source-area weighting or graph connectivity are novel to EOG;
- that a positive original A-Islands conditional-concordance result establishes realised movement or general predictive gain;
- that the adverse prospective A-Islands C−R3 result proves R3 is universally superior or causally explains the original concordance;
- that the adverse Tanzania result establishes universal superiority of current flow.

See `manuscript/submission/postoutcome_bibliographic_correction_2026-08-12.md` for the explicit Long et al. (2009) role correction and its timing relative to the frozen outcome.

# AI use statement — draft for NSO/Oikos disclosure

Generative AI tools, including OpenAI ChatGPT, were used as supportive tools during development of analysis code, repository documentation, manuscript drafting and language revision. All scientific decisions, ecological interpretations, inclusion and exclusion rules, confirmatory protocols, statistical results, code changes and final manuscript content were reviewed and accepted by the human author(s), who retain full responsibility for the accuracy, integrity and originality of the work. AI tools were not listed as authors and were not used to generate scientific images submitted as empirical evidence.

Before submission, the authors should verify the exact tool/model names and the scope of AI-assisted content against the final manuscript and repository history, and place the disclosure in the location requested by Oikos/NSO.

## Final conflict screen

Before submission, for every selected reviewer/editor candidate verify:

- no coauthorship with any manuscript author in the journal's relevant conflict window;
- no current institution shared with any manuscript author;
- no recent advisor–advisee relationship;
- no current grant or formal collaboration;
- no direct role in the source experiments or EOG analysis;
- no personal/professional conflict known to the authors.

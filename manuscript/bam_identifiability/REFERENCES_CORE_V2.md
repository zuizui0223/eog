# BAM inverse-identifiability — core references v2

## BAM / species-distribution foundations

Soberón, J. & Peterson, A.T. (2005).
Interpretation of Models of Fundamental Ecological Niches and Species' Distributional Areas.
Biodiversity Informatics 2.
DOI: 10.17161/bi.v2i0.4.

Soberón, J. & Nakamura, M. (2009).
Niches and distributional areas: Concepts, methods, and assumptions.
Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 106(Suppl. 2):19644–19650.
DOI: 10.1073/pnas.0901637106.
Use for niche/distribution conceptual distinctions.

Elith, J. & Leathwick, J.R. (2009).
Species Distribution Models: Ecological Explanation and Prediction Across Space and Time.
Annual Review of Ecology, Evolution, and Systematics 40:677–697.
DOI: 10.1146/annurev.ecolsys.110308.120159.
Use for the broad SDM prediction/explanation context.

Peterson, A.T., Papeş, M. & Soberón, J. (2015).
Mechanistic and Correlative Models of Ecological Niches.
European Journal of Ecology.
DOI: 10.1515/eje-2015-0014.

## Known-truth BAM / virtual species

Saupe, E.E., Barve, V., Myers, C.E., Soberón, J., Barve, N., Hensz, C.M.,
Peterson, A.T., Owens, H.L. & Lira-Noriega, A. (2012).
Variation in niche and distribution model performance: The need for a priori
assessment of key causal factors.
Ecological Modelling 237–238:11–22.
DOI: 10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2012.04.001.

Use explicitly to acknowledge that virtual species with known A/dispersal causal
configurations are prior art.

## Dynamic BAM

Soberón, J. & Osorio-Olvera, L. (2023).
A dynamic theory of the area of distribution.
Journal of Biogeography 50(6):1037–1048.
DOI: 10.1111/jbi.14587.

Use explicitly to acknowledge:

- dynamic BAM;
- movement/niche/biotic matrices;
- the prior observation that movement and niche effects can become difficult to
  disentangle.

## Absence information in BAM

Bariotakis, M. & Pirintsos, S.A. (2018).
Mapping absences within the BAM concept: Towards a new generation of ecological and
environmental indicators.
Ecological Indicators 90:564–568.
DOI: 10.1016/j.ecolind.2018.03.043.

Use to avoid claiming that absence information within BAM is new.

## Pattern-process equifinality / ecological non-identification

Yanco, S.W., McDevitt, A., Trueman, C.N., Hartley, L. & Wunder, M.B. (2020).
A modern method of multiple working hypotheses to improve inference in ecology.
Royal Society Open Science 7:200231.
DOI: 10.1098/rsos.200231.

Use for the general point that distinct ecological mechanisms can generate
indistinguishable patterns and that pre-data-collection modelling can expose
degenerate process-pattern relationships.

Lotterhos, K.E., Fitzpatrick, M.C. & Blackmon, H. (2022).
Simulation Tests of Methods in Evolution, Ecology, and Systematics: Pitfalls,
Progress, and Principles.
Annual Review of Ecology, Evolution, and Systematics 53:113–136.
DOI: 10.1146/annurev-ecolsys-102320-093722.

Use for:

- simulation-based method evaluation;
- equifinality as a generic inferential problem;
- retaining failed/refuted simulation results rather than validating only favorable
  cases.

## Generic equifinality / model sets

Beven, K. & Freer, J. (2001).
Equifinality, data assimilation, and uncertainty estimation in mechanistic modelling
of complex environmental systems using the GLUE methodology.
Journal of Hydrology 249:11–29.
DOI: 10.1016/S0022-1694(01)00421-8.

Use only as broad neighboring theory. Do not imply direct BAM precedence.

## Model-discrimination experiment design

Atkinson, A.C. & Cox, D.R. (1974).
Planning Experiments for Discriminating between Models.
Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B 36:321–334.
DOI: 10.1111/j.2517-6161.1974.tb01010.x.

Use to establish that experiment design for distinguishing competing models is
long-standing prior art.

Papanikolaou, N.E., Moffat, H., Fantinou, A., Perdikis, D.P., Bode, M. &
Drovandi, C. (2023).
Adaptive experimental design produces superior and more efficient estimates of
predator functional response.
PLOS ONE 18:e0288445.
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0288445.

Use as an ecology-specific example of adaptive design for model discrimination.

## Citation logic for this manuscript

The Introduction should use these references in three layers:

1. BAM forward theory:
   Soberón & Peterson 2005; Saupe et al. 2012; Soberón & Osorio-Olvera 2023.

2. Generic inverse ambiguity:
   Yanco et al. 2020; Lotterhos et al. 2022; optionally Beven & Freer 2001.

3. Evidence design prior art:
   Atkinson & Cox 1974; Papanikolaou et al. 2023.

Then state the narrower gap:

> prior work establishes BAM, equifinality and experimental design separately; the
> present contribution derives the exact BAM inverse survivor fibers under explicit
> evidence types, proves the positive-support inclusion ordering, and connects the
> resulting BAM-specific residual fiber to diagnostic measurement design.

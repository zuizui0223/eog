# Journal of Biogeography — cover letter draft

Dear Senior Editors,

We submit “Distributions are lossy intersections: exact identifiability limits for abiotic, biotic and movement mechanisms” as a Research Article. The study asks a biogeographic inverse question complementary to forward BAM theory: what can observed distributions actually identify about abiotic, biotic and movement mechanisms? Exact finite-set results and preregistered deterministic and independent stochastic simulations show that complete occurrence patterns can remain mechanistically non-identifying, and that stronger ecological restriction can increase positive-only ambiguity. The work connects these limits to targeted evidence design. We believe this conceptual advance will interest readers studying distributional mechanisms, accessibility and inference from geographic patterns.

Sincerely,

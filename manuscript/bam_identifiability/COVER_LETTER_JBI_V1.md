# Journal of Biogeography — cover letter draft

Dear Senior Editors,

We submit “Distributions are lossy intersections: exact identifiability limits for abiotic, biotic and movement mechanisms” as a Research Article. We ask a biogeographic inverse question complementary to forward BAM theory: what can distributions identify about the processes that generated them? Exact finite-set results and preregistered deterministic and independent stochastic simulations show that complete occurrence patterns can remain mechanistically non-identifying, and that stronger ecological restriction can increase positive-only ambiguity. The work converts these limits into targeted evidence design and should interest readers studying distributional mechanisms, accessibility and inference from geographic patterns.

Sincerely,

[Corresponding author]

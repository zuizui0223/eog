# Priority-claim rule after literature audit

The structural manuscript must avoid absolute priority language unless a future systematic search can substantiate it.

Use formulations such as:

- “We address a narrower validation question...”
- “In the literature reviewed here, we did not identify a directly equivalent design combining...”
- “The present contribution is the combined evidence architecture...”
- “Several individual components have established precedents.”

Avoid:

- “for the first time”;
- “no previous study”;
- “existing models do not...” unless immediately scoped to a specific model family/reference;
- “novel” when it refers only to graph connectivity, occupied-source distance, threshold ensembles, suitability-connectivity integration or uncertainty analysis.

This rule is intentionally stronger than the evidence required to publish the paper: the manuscript can make a meaningful contribution without an absolute first-in-literature claim.

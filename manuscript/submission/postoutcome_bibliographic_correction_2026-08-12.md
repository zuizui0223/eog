# Post-outcome bibliographic correction — Long et al. 2009

Date recorded: **2026-08-12**

## Correction

The pre-outcome island-isolation audit described Long, Trussell & Elliman (2009), DOI `10.1890/08-1337.1`, as evidence for explicit stepping-stone isolation. A post-outcome publisher/source recheck showed that this role was incorrect.

The paper is:

> Long, J.D., Trussell, G.C. & Elliman, T. (2009). Linking invasions and biogeography: Isolation differentially affects exotic and native plant diversity. *Ecology* 90: 863–868. DOI `10.1890/08-1337.1`.

Its island analysis evaluates island area and isolation from the mainland across 25 Boston Harbor islands. It supports the established importance of recipient size and mainland isolation, but it is **not** the source for the project's generic stepping-stone term.

## Why the pre-outcome scientific design is not changed

This correction was discovered **after** the one-time `C−R3` outcome had been executed. The historical pre-outcome contract is therefore left unchanged rather than rewritten retrospectively.

The stepping-stone/network-position rationale in the frozen R3 reference did not depend uniquely on Long et al. (2009). Before the outcome, the same audit explicitly identified:

- Weigelt & Kreft (2013), DOI `10.1111/j.1600-0587.2012.07669.x`, for multidimensional isolation including surrounding land and stepping-stone representations;
- Sillero, Biaggini & Corti (2018), DOI `10.1093/biolinnean/bly033`, **Analysing the importance of stepping-stone islands in maintaining structural connectivity and endemicity**, for graph-theoretic stepping-stone connectivity;
- Carter, Perry & Russell (2020), DOI `10.1111/jbi.13778`, for the empirical dimensions of mainland distance, stepping stones and insular network position.

Accordingly, this is a **bibliographic-role correction**, not a change to R3, C, the graph radii, taxa, endpoint, fit engine or interpretation of the frozen outcome. The authoritative result is not rerun.

## Submission rule

All submission-facing prose and reference ledgers must use Long et al. (2009) only for island area/mainland-isolation context. Stepping-stone claims must be supported by the correctly matched references above. The historical pre-outcome document remains part of the archived audit trail together with this correction, so the timing and nature of the error are visible.

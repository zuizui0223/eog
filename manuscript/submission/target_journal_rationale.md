# Target-journal rationale after novelty audit

## Ecological Informatics — first target

The manuscript is best treated as an informatics/validation paper rather than a claim of a newly invented connectivity algorithm. Its strongest target-journal features are:

- explicit computational separation of estimands;
- leakage-controlled structural feature construction;
- multi-species empirical benchmarking;
- strong-reference model comparison;
- uncertainty and applicability accounting;
- source, result and manuscript-artifact provenance;
- an offline reproducible submission package.

This is aligned with a journal focused on computational ecology, ecological data modelling, uncertainty analysis and species-distribution modelling.

Recent papers in the journal make the topic fit clear but also raise the novelty bar: Kim et al. 2024 couples landscape connectivity with incidence-function/species-distribution modelling, and Cushman et al. 2026 compares 171 connectivity-model combinations and source/threshold assumptions. Those papers must be treated as context, not ignored.

## Why the adverse Tanzania result belongs in the first submission

Removing Tanzania would make the manuscript look like another paper showing that a connectivity variable improves a local-only model. Keeping Tanzania changes the scientific question from “can connectivity help?” to “when does a generic structural term add information relative to what the reference already knows?” That is the more defensible and more informative paper.

## Why not Methods in Ecology and Evolution first

Current MEE guidance is unfavorable to the present version because:

- Research Articles are expected to describe new methods;
- linked existing-method workflows generally do not qualify as new methods;
- computational methods normally should be tested with simulations or benchmark datasets;
- the newer Workflow type expects substantial quantifiable improvements, error/uncertainty analysis and extensive sensitivity/benchmark testing.

EOG may later support a separate MEE-oriented paper after prospective simulation/ablation/theory development. The present two-real-dataset structural paper should not be stretched into that claim.

## Backup logic

If Ecological Informatics rejects for editorial novelty/fit without identifying a validity flaw, prefer retargeting to Ecological Modelling or an appropriate landscape-ecology outlet over reopening the frozen analyses. If a reviewer/editor identifies a concrete validity-threatening omitted comparator, preregister its addition relative to this audit before inspecting the new outcome.

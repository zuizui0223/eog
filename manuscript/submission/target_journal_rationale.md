# Target-journal rationale after the island-isolation novelty audit

## Current state

There is **no unconditional first journal before the prospectively frozen island-isolation adequacy result is known**. The journal route is selected by a predeclared scientific decision rule, not by post-result preference.

## Route A — Journal of Biogeography if a residual island increment survives R3

If the frozen A-Islands `C − R3` matched held-out log-loss contrast is robustly negative, *Journal of Biogeography* becomes the preferred first target.

That result would support a genuinely biogeographic question: whether generic multidimensional island isolation is structurally sufficient for species occurrence after controlling recipient climate and area, continental-mainland distance, direct and area-weighted occupied-source pressure, surrounding landmass, generic stepping-stone accessibility and generic island-network position.

The manuscript should then lead with the island-isolation problem rather than with EOG as software or a generic connectivity algorithm. Carter, Perry & Russell (2020; DOI `10.1111/jbi.13778`) is the closest multidimensional-isolation comparator and must be discussed prominently.

The journal currently emphasizes theoretical foundations, conceptual advances, broadly relevant biogeographic questions, rigorous analysis and clear argument. A robust residual species-conditioned archipelago increment would meet that bar more naturally than a generic connectivity-tool claim.

Tanzania remains useful in this route as a non-island strong-reference stress test showing that a generic structural term is not universally additive.

## Route B — Ecological Informatics if R3 absorbs the island increment

If `C − R3` is null, adverse or indeterminate, do not weaken R3. *Ecological Informatics* becomes the preferred first target.

The manuscript is then best treated as an informatics/validation paper whose main contributions are:

- explicit separation of structural estimands;
- leakage-controlled construction of response-derived source features;
- reference-conditioned held-out testing;
- multi-species empirical benchmarking;
- strong-reference falsification;
- uncertainty and applicability accounting;
- source, result and manuscript-artifact provenance.

The island result becomes a structural-saturation test: the original signal beyond climate + nearest source is evaluated against a substantially richer conventional-isolation reference, regardless of whether anything remains.

## Why Methods in Ecology and Evolution remains a no-go for this empirical version

The current paper still assembles established graph/connectivity components into a distinctive validation architecture rather than introducing a clearly new general method primitive. A separate prospective MEE-oriented paper would need known-truth simulations, calibration/error analysis, formal ablation and broad reusable methodological evidence.

## Backup logic

For the broader computational route, *Ecological Modelling* remains a reasonable backup if *Ecological Informatics* rejects on editorial fit rather than a validity flaw.

Journal retargeting must never be used as a reason to delete Tanzania, weaken the island R3 comparator, change the frozen extension result, or add a post-outcome positive benchmark.

# Editorial positioning for the structural/island-isolation paper

The final pitch is selected after the frozen A-Islands `C − R3` result. The acronym EOG should not lead either route.

## Core scientific problem

Existing island biogeography already recognizes mainland distance, stepping stones, surrounding landmass and insular network position as distinct components of isolation. The unresolved question tested here is whether that generic multidimensional isolation description is **structurally sufficient for a focal species**, once the target's position relative to observed source islands is evaluated without held-out response leakage.

## Core methodological contribution

EOG provides a reference-conditioned held-out probe: response-derived source features are reconstructed from outer-training occurrences only, conventional generic isolation is represented explicitly, and the final species-conditioned archipelago term earns a claim only if it adds information beyond that declared reference.

## Route A pitch — robust residual island increment

Target: **Journal of Biogeography**.

One-sentence pitch:

> We test whether multidimensional island isolation is sufficient for species occurrence by separating recipient environment and area, mainland distance, source pressure and source landmass, surrounding landmass, generic stepping-stone accessibility and generic network position from a final species-conditioned occupied-source archipelago term across 886 plant taxa and 842 Australian continental islands.

The editor should see immediately that Carter et al. 2020, Weigelt & Kreft 2013, Long 2009, Sillero et al. 2018 and Daniel et al. 2023 are acknowledged as direct precedents rather than obscured.

Tanzania remains a secondary non-island strong-reference boundary showing that generic structural additions are not universally useful.

## Route B pitch — no residual island increment

Target: **Ecological Informatics**.

One-sentence pitch:

> We develop a leakage-safe structural-adequacy framework that asks whether occurrence-conditioned landscape configuration adds held-out information after progressively stronger reference content, retaining a broad positive island signal beyond climate+nearest source, a stronger multidimensional-isolation saturation test, and an adverse current-flow benchmark without post-hoc rescue.

## Desk-reject defense

If an editor sees the work as another connectivity index, the manuscript has failed to communicate the actual question. The Introduction must begin with established island-isolation/connectivity precedents and then state the **reference-conditioned adequacy problem**.

Do not respond to novelty risk by weakening R3, adding a post-result positive dataset, selecting taxa, or tuning graph scales. The frozen result determines the journal route.

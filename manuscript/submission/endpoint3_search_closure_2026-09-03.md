# EOG-WF endpoint-3 search closure — 2026-09-03

Chapter: **N3 / EOG — WHERE can it become real?**  
Spine: **住める場所と、辿り着ける場所は違う。**

## Decision

The fresh-candidate search for this manuscript is closed **without obtaining a third scored predictive endpoint**.

This is an anti-bias stopping decision, not a predictive result. It does not convert an unobserved third endpoint into a null/adverse result and does not convert protocol STOPs into failures of Layer B.

## Evidence at closure

Two genuinely fresh paired predictive endpoints reached valid scores under the unchanged two-layer architecture:

1. Azores yellow eel telemetry — favorable paired complementarity, augmented arm 5/5 heldout-block wins;
2. Southwest Louisiana King Rail passive acoustics — favorable paired complementarity, augmented arm 7/8 heldout-occasion wins.

The manuscript candidate funnel additionally contains **25 protocol-integrity STOPs** and **2 administrative exclusions**. The explicitly frozen final source-architecture shortlist — MICA muskrat, Australian cassowary, Norway Willow Warbler — was exhausted with all three attempts stopping before biological response/model scoring.

Current scientific status therefore remains:

`replicated_candidate_general_predictive_complement`

The third predictive replication is `unresolved_not_obtained`.

## Why the search stops here

The original endpoint-3 programme was intended to provide one additional heterogeneous replication under strict prospective source, registry, effort, structural and response-access rules. Once the frozen shortlist was exhausted, continuing to screen new systems until one happened to survive data-interface gates would change a bounded replication programme into open-ended data-availability hunting.

The correct response is to stop the search, retain all STOPs in the denominator, and write the paper at the evidence level actually achieved.

## Claim ceiling

Supported:

> A label-invariant summary of surviving accessibility-compatible worlds contained non-redundant heldout predictive information beyond the same strong conventional learner in two genuinely fresh heterogeneous observation systems.

Not supported:

- universal predictive superiority;
- standalone Layer-B superiority;
- a third-system replication;
- causal identification;
- truth of any exact Layer-A world;
- recovered dispersal history;
- confirmation of a particular movement/connectivity mechanism from a favorable Layer-B result.

## Publication consequence

The Nature Ecology & Evolution trigger for this manuscript is closed because the prospectively required third favorable predictive endpoint was not obtained.

Primary target: **Methods in Ecology and Evolution**.

Conditional fallback: **Ecography**, only if the final paper is more naturally framed as biogeographic/spatiotemporal inference than as a general ecological method.

Journal choice may change exposition but may not reopen candidate search, alter frozen endpoints, repair stopped attempts, or add a favorable dataset for prestige.

## Manuscript evidence spine

1. **Architecture** — local viability is not the same object as accessibility-compatible realization; Layer A retains exact world compatibility/falsification, Layer B exposes a label-invariant predictive summary.
2. **Prospective funnel** — show the complete search process: 2 scored endpoints, 25 protocol STOPs, 2 administrative exclusions.
3. **Paired performance** — show Azores and Louisiana separately; no pooled significance or universal-benefit claim.
4. **Layer-A / Layer-B decoupling** — use Louisiana to show that strong world falsification and small predictive complementarity are distinct inferential objects.

The candidate funnel is part of the method result: strict prospective data-interface requirements can prevent many apparently attractive ecological datasets from yielding an auditable predictive test.

## Hard stop

For this manuscript programme:

- no new fresh candidates;
- no fourth/prestige dataset;
- no repairs of terminal candidates;
- no new generic connectivity operator;
- no promotion from post-hoc Azores/Louisiana robustness;
- no endpoint-3 placebo/excluded-world upgrade because endpoint-3 response was never opened.

Next phase: **manuscript and submission-package assembly**.

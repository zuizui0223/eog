# Why the current empirical evidence is sufficient for first submission

## Positive evidence is broad, not isolated

The A-Islands primary estimate is 0.6177465917820878 against a 0.5 conditional-concordance null, with a species-bootstrap interval of 0.6086806094469824–0.626944450492123. Of 845 estimable taxa, 672 are above the null, 42 equal it and 131 are below it. The primary analysis therefore has both an aggregate effect and broad species-level directionality.

## The positive estimand is already narrow enough to be interpretable

A-Islands compares occupied and unoccupied held-out islands only within frozen strata of pointwise climatic support and nearest outer-training occurrence distance. It does not claim overall SDM improvement or superiority over a matrix-aware connectivity model. This makes its result scientifically useful even after recognizing strong prior literature on source-conditioned and connectivity-augmented occurrence models.

## The external benchmark can falsify the generic structural increment

Tanzania is not a second easy positive example. Its reference already includes patch area, species/fold-adaptive matrix-aware current flow, their interaction and nearest training occurrence. The additional EOG term is adverse under primary LOSO (+0.032113119 log loss), with 43 of 60 species adverse. This establishes that the generic structural term does not automatically add predictive information after a strong connectivity reference.

## Sensitivity uncertainty is visible

Tanzania spatial-block intervals cross zero and non-estimability increases. The manuscript does not convert this to a positive or negative confirmation. That reduces the external generalization strength but improves the credibility of the stated boundary.

## What is missing is not another result

The latest literature audit shows that the main pre-submission risk is overbroad novelty framing. Existing studies already cover source-conditioned distance, source-quality connectivity, habitat-network occurrence prediction, functional-habitat integration, threshold sensitivity and connectivity uncertainty. The scientifically appropriate response is to narrow the contribution to the leakage-safe, reference-conditioned held-out validation architecture, not to tune the outcomes until another favourable comparison appears.

## Decision

The frozen results are sufficient for first submission once the closest-prior framing revision and ordinary human/release gates are complete.

## Publication principle

Journal choice follows the **scientific claim actually supported after endpoint 3**. It must never be used to rescue, retune, relabel, or selectively omit an endpoint.

The highest journal route is attempted only when the result supports a broad ecological inference principle, not merely because three datasets are favorable.

## Route A — highest-challenge ecology route

### Trigger

Attempt a high-selectivity broad-ecology submission, with **Nature Ecology & Evolution** or an **Ecology Letters** Letter as the first editorial challenge, only if all of the following hold prospectively without rescue tuning:

- endpoint 3 reaches a valid predictive terminal decision and is favorable;
- all three fresh systems used the unchanged Layer-B representation and a strong same-learner baseline;
- the systems are materially heterogeneous in ecology and observation process;
- the frozen cross-ecosystem synthesis supports the interpretation that accessibility/world-state information provides a non-redundant predictive axis beyond local/site covariates;
- the secondary ten-feature placebo does not make the real Layer-B gain look like a generic dimensionality effect;
- the manuscript can be written around a general ecological statement rather than around a software architecture or catalogue of operators;
- the wording remains below universal superiority, exact-mechanism truth, causal identification, or recovered dispersal history.

### Intended claim level

A suitable high-level claim would be of the form:

> Local suitability and the changing set of accessibility-compatible worlds can encode distinct predictive information about occurrence.

The contribution must be the ecological inference principle, with EOG as the testable implementation of that principle.

### Submission sequence

1. Nature Ecology & Evolution **or** Ecology Letters, chosen from the final manuscript emphasis;
2. if rejected primarily on breadth/selectivity rather than a scientific flaw, retarget promptly to Methods in Ecology and Evolution without adding another favorable system merely to improve journal rank.

## Route B — Methods in Ecology and Evolution main route

### Trigger

Use **Methods in Ecology and Evolution** as the default strong route if endpoint 3 is:

- favorable but the result is best framed as methodological rather than field-wide ecological principle;
- null, leaving two favorable systems and one neutral system;
- heterogeneous in effect size but still supports a reproducible, auditable predictive-complement framework;
- adverse in a way that preserves a useful Layer-A structural/falsification method while showing that Layer-B predictive value is context dependent.

### Intended claim level

The paper centers on a prospectively testable two-layer method:

- exact compatible-world identity retained for sequential update/falsification;
- label-invariant world-set summary used for prediction;
- strong paired comparator;
- once-only outcome access;
- explicit favorable/null/adverse/non-estimable/STOP semantics;
- no post-outcome world redesign.

A null or adverse third predictive endpoint is not a reason to hide heterogeneity. It becomes part of the method's applicability boundary.

## Route C — Ecography

Use **Ecography** when the final manuscript is strongest as a biogeographic/spatiotemporal inference paper rather than a general methods paper, especially if endpoint 3 reveals interpretable context dependence in when accessibility-state information adds predictive value.

Do not force this route by making unsupported claims about movement mechanism or realized dispersal.

## Source/non-estimable STOP route

A source, registry, geometry, temporal-context, structural-scale, or non-estimable STOP before the predictive endpoint:

- is methods-integrity evidence;
- is not favorable, null, or adverse predictive evidence;
- does not lower the journal target by itself;
- does not authorize repair of the same consumed candidate;
- permits continuation only to a scientifically distinct fresh endpoint if issue #141's frozen rules allow it.

The candidate funnel and STOP certificates belong in the paper/supplement regardless of final journal.

## Development stop rule

After a valid third fresh predictive terminal endpoint, frozen synthesis, placebo robustness, and paper figures are complete:

- stop adding generic connectivity operators;
- stop seeking extra favorable datasets for prestige;
- freeze the claim at the predeclared endpoint-3 decision boundary;
- move directly to manuscript/package preparation.

A journal rejection may motivate exposition, figure, robustness, or positioning revisions, but must not reopen consumed scientific outcomes.

## Citation-level claim boundary

The revised manuscript may claim that the **combined EOG validation design** is distinctive: separate estimands, outer-training-only response-derived source features, predeclared scenario aggregation without held-out winner selection, matched held-out incremental evaluation, a strong-reference falsification benchmark, explicit failures, retained positive/adverse results and source-to-manuscript auditability.

For the island route, the stronger conditional claim is narrower: the project tests whether **species-conditioned occupied-source archipelago configuration** retains held-out information after conventional generic island-isolation axes, island/source landmass, multi-source pressure and generic network embedding have already been represented.

It must not claim priority for multidimensional island isolation, mainland isolation, stepping stones, surrounding land area, insular network position, graph-based island connectivity, empirical graph validation, accessibility, source-conditioned proximity/connectivity, source-quality/area weighting, suitability-derived resistance/connectivity, suitability-plus-connectivity integration, occupied-neighbour predictors, habitat-network occurrence models, threshold sensitivity, uncertainty propagation, current flow, or graph topology.

## Already present and still required

The current manuscript already cites Ortiz-Rodríguez et al. (2019), DOI `10.1002/ece3.5567`, which combines local habitat suitability, weighted connectivity and habitat-network topology to predict patch occurrence. It remains a central closest comparator and should be discussed alongside the additions above rather than as the sole integrated-network precedent.

## Citation-level claim boundary

The revised manuscript may claim that the **combined EOG validation design** is distinctive: separate estimands, outer-training-only anchors, predeclared scenario aggregation, matched held-out incremental evaluation, a strong-reference falsification benchmark, explicit failures, retained positive/adverse results and source-to-manuscript auditability.

It must not claim priority for accessibility, suitability-plus-connectivity integration, occupied-neighbour predictors, habitat-network occurrence models, threshold sensitivity, uncertainty propagation, current flow, or graph topology.
